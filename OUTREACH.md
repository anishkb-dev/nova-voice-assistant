## 5. 60-second demo video script (film this on your phone — it’s your best asset)

Recruiters skim. A working demo beats any bullet point. Screen-record or phone-film, ~60s, no editing needed:

1. **0–5s** — You, to camera: *“This is NOVA — a voice assistant I built from scratch that controls my room.”*
2. **5–20s** — Say *“Hey Nova, light up the room.”* → show the lamp turn on. Then *“set the room to 21.”* → mention the AC responds.
3. **20–35s** — Show the dashboard: live temperature / humidity / motion updating.
4. **35–50s** — Quick screen flash of the architecture (README diagram): mic → Whisper → LLM → MQTT → ESP32.
5. **50–60s** — *“Built solo — firmware to LLM. Code’s on my GitHub.”*

Upload unlisted to YouTube or a Loom; drop the link in your resume, LinkedIn, and every DM above.
